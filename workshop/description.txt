[h1]HITMAN CONTRACTS OVERHAUL[/h1]
[b]Drones. Moving targets. Social stealth. A second mission is now hiding inside your mission.[/b]

HCO adds optional high-value-target operations to compatible Intravenous 2 missions. A protected executive, broker, commander or fixer enters the map with close guards, a response force and airborne security. Find the intelligence, locate the moving target and decide how the operation ends.

Infiltrate in a stolen uniform, disable the drones and strike quietly—or start a firefight and watch the map become a coordinated hunt. Contracts live inside the normal campaign through native objectives, actors, interactions, weapons and saves.

[h2]THE CONTRACT[/h2]

Compatible missions generate one to three deterministic optional contracts. The target is not revealed for free.

[olist]
[*][b]Recover intelligence.[/b] Find the marked dead drop.
[*][b]Identify the target.[/b] Intel reveals a live moving marker.
[*][b]Study the protection.[/b] Five close guards protect the principal; additional units search and attack.
[*][b]Choose an approach.[/b] Use a disguise, isolate the target, disrupt security or fight.
[*][b]Finish the contract.[/b] Neutralize the target, collect campaign money and continue the mission.
[/olist]

Contracts and rewards persist across reloads. Completed contracts cannot pay twice; failed unpaid attempts can produce a fresh operation later.

[h2]TARGETS THAT ACTUALLY MOVE[/h2]

HCO builds a deterministic five-to-eight-point routine from safe native patrol sectors. The principal uses the game's pathfinding and doors while close protection follows nearby.

Nearby unsuppressed gunfire creates caution without identifying the shooter. Injured guards, casualties or confirmed contact drive the principal toward safety. Under pressure the detail can shelter or physically evacuate the target—never teleport it.

Four archetypes alter appearance, reward, protection and drone doctrine. Difficulty scales health, response size, sensors, drone pressure, threat rating and payout together.

[h2]DISGUISES AND SOCIAL STEALTH[/h2]

Dead or unconscious characters can provide Staff, Security or Elite Security disguises through the normal body menu. A cyan stitch marker shows an available uniform. Taking it changes your visible identity and can transfer native keycard credentials.

A disguise permits plausible behavior. It does not make you permanently invisible.

[list]
[*]Normal walking and any carried weapon can preserve cover at a believable distance.
[*]Running, aiming, firing, lockpicking, carrying bodies and restricted access build suspicion.
[*]Close colleagues and elite guards inspect unfamiliar faces faster.
[*]Sustained close scrutiny can reveal the deception; point-blank recognition is especially dangerous.
[*]An unseen shot creates an investigation at its location without identifying the shooter.
[*]A witness can start a radio report. Interrupt it before recognition becomes global compromise.
[*]Finding the body that supplied your uniform can compromise that disguise across the network.
[/list]

A cyan shimmer confirms active cover and turns red when compromised. Use [b]Restore original identity[/b] in a body menu to remove the disguise; stolen credentials remain.

[h2]A PHYSICAL DRONE SECURITY NETWORK[/h2]

Drones exist before combat, patrol valid outdoor areas and maintain security around active contracts. When an alarm clears, survivors return to patrol.

The roster contains seven physical airframes:

[list]
[*][b]Scout[/b] — unarmed surveillance and network relay.
[*][b]Light Pistol / SMG / Laser[/b] — fast and fragile.
[*][b]Heavy Pistol / SMG / Laser[/b] — slower, armored weapons platforms.
[/list]

Each model has its own silhouette, scale, rotor sound, movement, sensor and wreck. Search cones use an independent gimbal. Pistols and SMGs fire native projectiles; lasers expose an aim line and charge window before discharge.

The network shares confirmed sightings and observed appearance, not supernatural knowledge. A clean distant disguise creates amber suspicion. Sustained close cone-and-raycast contact is required for confirmation. Changing identity invalidates an old match; overt behavior restores rapid detection.

Drones are real targets. Light airframes usually fall to one hit; heavy models take roughly two or three depending on the weapon. Electronic disruption also works. Destroyed drones lose cone and weapons, tumble with sparks and smoke, then remain as evidence-producing wrecks.

A drone may cross a narrow verified exterior barrier only after steering fails. During the hop it cannot scan, relay or fire. Buildings, void and unsafe edges remain forbidden. Fleet limits allow seven drones per contract and twelve across the map.

[h2]SECURITY ESCALATION[/h2]

HCO separates noise, evidence and confirmed identity.

[list]
[*]Suspicious sound creates a local investigation.
[*]A body or wounded guard raises pressure and can request drones.
[*]Three unsuppressed player shots within eight seconds trigger aggression; suppressed shots and NPC fire do not.
[*]Confirmed contact shares last position and observed identity.
[*]The principal and close guards move toward safety while response units hunt the player.
[*]Once evidence and threat clear, the network stands down and can react again.
[/list]

Security can know where something happened without knowing the disguised person walking past caused it.

[h2]NATIVE-FIRST DESIGN[/h2]

There is no HCO hotkey, detached screen, invented currency or permanent overlay. The mod uses native objectives, markers, body actions, patrols, radios, credentials, projectiles, damage and campaign persistence.

Unsafe mission states and story-critical actors are skipped. The original mission stays active.

[h2]HOW TO PLAY[/h2]

Start a compatible mission [b]from the beginning[/b]. If enough safe actors exist, the intelligence objective appears beneath the normal objective.

Recover the intel and follow the moving target. Mid-mission saves may require a mission restart.
