[h2]NATIVE-FIRST DESIGN[/h2]
There is no cheat menu, F-key overlay, separate currency, or external progression screen. A compatible mission creates one optional contract automatically. Unknown or unsafe missions are skipped instead of risking story actors or vanilla objectives.

The target and security team do not receive live omniscient player coordinates. Knowledge comes from direct sightings, nearby warnings, radios, bodies, and sensors, then decays into distributed search sectors.

[h2]HOW DISGUISES WORK[/h2]
Use the normal body interaction selector and choose [b]Search body / take disguise[/b]. Walking normally with suitable equipment is convincing. Running, aiming, lockpicking, carrying bodies, firing, or showing the wrong weapon raises suspicion. Close colleagues and elite guards inspect you more quickly.

When the matching body is discovered, knowledge starts locally. A real NPC radio must finish its report before the uniform becomes globally compromised — jam the radio or stop the carrier to contain the information.

[h2]COMPATIBILITY[/h2]
Built for Intravenous 2 [b]1.4.12HF3[/b]. Hideout, tutorial, prologue, intro, outro, transition, and unsafe story situations are deliberately skipped. Generic maps need enough safe patrolling goons to qualify.

Install only one HCO copy. If a local and Workshop copy are both active, the first-loaded version wins. HCO is separate from the Intravenous 2 Cheat Trainer and does not modify its files.
